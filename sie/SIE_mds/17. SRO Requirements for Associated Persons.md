**Associated Person (FINRA)**
- Officer, director, partner or branch manager of a member firm
- Employee of the member firm unless they are only clerical/ministerial
- Persons engaged in IB or securities businesses controlled by member firm

Most associated persons of a registered broker-dealer must register w/ FINRA based on the type of business the firm does or they type of securities they handle.

 two levels of qualification and registration:
 - **Registered representatives**: generally sales personnel engaged in securities business activities. **(Must complete Series 7)**
 - **Principals**: generally officers and other management or supervisory personnel who are involved in day-to-day management or operations **(Must complete Series 24)**

**Non Associated Persons**: May not have direct contact with customers. They may not receive finders fees or commissions from RRs for finding clients. They may only (they must be supervised closely during these):
- Give invitations to firm events
- Inquire whether they wish to discuss investments with a RR
- Inquire whether they want to receive investment literature from the firm

They may not:
- Accept customer orders (occasionally transcribing details submitted by customers is okay as long as registered person reviews orders before they are entered)

**Failure**: 30 day waiting period between 1-2 and 2-3** attempts, then 180 days afterwards

**Supervision of RRs**: RRs and APs must be supervised. Member firms must:
- Preserve records of carrying out supervisory procedures
- Review and endore all transactions of RR (and all correspondence)
- Approve and review customer accounts periodically 
- Inspect office/physical locations once per year
- Ascertain good character of all registered persons

Everybody must have a supervisor who has passed the right regulatory exams. Any questions about rules go to them, they are responsible for who they supervise.

**How to Register**: Form U4 is required (Uniform Application for Securities Industry Registration or Transfer). Fingerprints added to FINRA CRD. This is your permanent record in the financial world. Form U4 has questions on:
- Legal issues
- Terminations/disciplinary actions at work
- Customer complaints
- Financial events (bankruptcies, liens, compromises with creditors)
Primary residence foreclosure doesn't go on record

**Statutory Disqualification**: A firm may not hire someone with a SD unless FINRA provides permission. This can happen due to:
- Being expelled from an SRO
- Having registration denied, suspended or revoked by SEC or regulatory agency like CFTC or foreign entity
- Violating securities law, commodities law etc.
- Failing to supervise someone they were meant to unless there was a system that should have caught transgression and the supervisor discharged their duties to that system
- Felony within last 10 years or a misdemeanor involving investments and related financial crimes (even if pardoned, it must be submitted on U4)

To hire someone with an SD, a firm files an application with FINRA requesting permission (*eligability proceeding*) and FINRA's department of member regulation evaluates it and makes a recommendation to the National Adjudicatory Council (NAC).

They may request heightened supervision through a supervisory plan tailored to the specific SD person.

**Form U5**: Form given from departing firm about reason for termination and any derogatory marks if they exist

**Firms must background check and reasonably verify U4**

**Fingerprinting**: if a person comes into contact with funds, securities, or the firm’s books and records, the fingerprinting requirement applies.

**State Registration (Blue-Sky Rules)**: RRs must register with each state they do business in. They must also pass Series 63, Series 65, Series 66. Securities must also be registered in all states involved.

**Continuing Education**
- **Regulatory Element**: Basically online, annual training that must be done to maintain registration for each registration they have
- **Firm Element**: Training made by firm, done yearly, contents must be kept

**Military Duty**: prevents you from being inactive registration and continuing education requirements. You may not be able to function as RRs or contact new customers, but you can make money off of existing ones

**Maintaining Qualifications Program**: If you quit but want to keep your qualifications, just pay $100 a year for this and do the CE requirements yearly, lasts 5 years. Must meet these:
1. They must have been registered in the terminated registration category for at least one year immediately prior to the termination in that category.
2. They must elect to participate in the MQP within two years from the date of termination.
3. They must complete all of their CE requirements by their due dates.
4. They cannot have been CE Inactive for two consecutive years.
5. They cannot be subject to statutory disqualification.
