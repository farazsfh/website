
**Arbitration Disclosure**: Signing Form U4 means you agree to arbitration as the process for resolving firm, customer, associated persons disputes
- Any disputes -> you cant sue them in court
- Employment discrimination -> must be arbitrated under FINRA rules, if parties agree
- Whistleblower -> not required to arbitrate under FINRA, can do so if all parties agree
- Arbitration awards are generally FINAL
- Obtaining documents/evidence is limited
- Arbitrators are not required to explain decisions unless a join request for it has been submitted by all parties 20 days prior to first hearing date
- Arbitrators could be anybody (securities industry or not)
- There are time limits for brining a claim to arbitration, in some cases, if ineligible, you can bring to court

**Waivers**: A disqualified person may apply for a waiver from a SRO to enter/re-enter the securities industry -> eligibility proceeding -> they notify SEC -> SEC can overturn -> if not, heightened supervision

**Form U4**: must be reviewed in 30 days after filed
**Form U5**: must be reviewed in 60 days after filed for registration, must be provided in 2 business days of request

**FINRA CRD (Central Registration Depository)**: Database with disciplinary histories, employment histories of securities persons

**Updating Form U4**: If you are charged with a felony OR failure to appear for felony hearing OR a misdemeanor of financial nature, you must update U4 promptly. If you're arested but not charged, no need to update.

**Form U5**: Form that a member firm must submit to FINRA within 30 days (and notify employee). This form details the reasons for termination. Customer complaints are put here if received by firm even after person has left.

**Form U6**: Used by regulators to report disciplinary actions against RRs and/or firms. Reports arbitration awards against as well. Also fed into CRD

**BrokerCheck**: FINRAs public disclosure program
- Can view disciplinary history of member firms or RRs, legal charges as well
- Info on everyone registered or has been registered for the last 10 years
- Can view employment history
- Custom arbitrations, settlements, complaints
- Investigations
- Terminations of employment after financial investigation
If there are any discrepancies on BrokerCheck, you must file an amended U4 with FINRA
If you're not currently registered with FINRA, then you must fill out Broker Comment Request Form

**Investor Education**: Firms must provide customers these yearly:
- FINRA’s BrokerCheck hotline number
- FINRA website address
- A statement regarding the availability of an investor brochure that includes information describing FINRA’s BrokerCheck
Must also include a hyperlink to BrokerCheck on first customer landing page (if you hold customer accounts)
- MSRB has investor education rules yearly:
	- That the regulated entity is registered with the MSRB and the SEC
	- The MSRB’s website address
	- That there’s a brochure (_Investor Brochure_) available on the MSRB’s website which describes the protections available under MSRB rules as well as the process by which a complaint may be filed with the appropriate regulatory authority

**Expungement**: If information in CRD system is incorrect, you may make report iF:
- Claim is factually impossible or erroneous
- You weren't involved in it
- Claim is false

**Complaints**: Any written statement by a customer or on behalf of a customer alleging upsetness about activities of a person under control of member firm regarding solicitation or execution of a transaction
- If received, must be forwarded to supervising principal
- Firms have to maintain separate file of all written complaints including correspondence for 4 years. Response to complaint may be written or oral. Complaint file must be kept for everyone (even if empty)in
